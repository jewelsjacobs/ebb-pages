A symptom tracker you can use on your worst days

Most symptom trackers assume you have energy. Long forms, dozens of fields, charts to configure. On a flare day — when the pain is loud and the brain fog is thick — that's exactly when logging matters most and exactly when you can't face a five-minute form.

Ebb was built around that reality. You tap your symptoms, rate each one 1 to 5, add a note if you want, and you're done. Thirty seconds, even with brain fog.

Why track fibromyalgia symptoms at all?

Two reasons, and both are practical, not aspirational:

  • Appointments. "How have you been since last visit?" is an impossible question with brain fog. A trend chart of your pain, fatigue, and sleep over the last three months answers it in one glance — and Ebb exports to PDF so you can hand it to your doctor.
  • Patterns. Flares rarely come from nowhere. Consistent, tiny logs — not perfect ones — are how you spot that sleep dips two days before pain spikes, or that a med change actually helped.

What makes tracking sustainable with chronic illness

The tracker you'll still be using in six months is the one that costs the least energy. Ebb keeps the cost low on purpose:

  • One screen, minimal taps. No navigating menus to log.

  • A gentle daily reminder at whatever time works for your body.
  • Missed days don't break anything. No streaks, no guilt. Log when you can.

Common questions

What fibromyalgia symptoms can I track in Ebb?

Whatever you name. Most people with fibro track widespread pain, fatigue, brain fog, sleep quality, morning stiffness, headaches, and mood. You rate each one 1 to 5 and can add one short note for the day.

Is Ebb free?

Yes. The free tier lets you track up to 5 symptoms with full daily logging, complete history, 7-day trends, a daily reminder, and CSV export. Premium ($4.99/month or $29.99/year) adds unlimited symptoms, full trend ranges, and doctor-ready PDF reports.

Can I show my log to my rheumatologist?

Yes. Premium generates a PDF report of the last 30 days — your daily 1–5 ratings and your notes, laid out as a table a clinician can skim (see a real sample). The free tier exports your full history as CSV.

What happens if I miss days during a flare?

Nothing. Ebb has no streaks and no guilt mechanics. A gap of a week doesn't break your history or your trends — you pick up whenever you can.
