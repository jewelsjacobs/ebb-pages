A symptom tracker for days when standing up is the hard part

Most symptom trackers assume you have energy and focus to spare. With POTS, the days you most need to log — the pre-syncope mornings, the heart-pounding afternoons — are exactly the days a five-minute form is out of reach.

Ebb was built around that reality. You tap your symptoms, rate each one 1 to 5, add a note if you want, and you're done. Thirty seconds, even mid-flare.

Why track POTS symptoms at all?

Two reasons, and both are practical, not aspirational:

  • Appointments. Dysautonomia symptoms scatter across weeks — dizziness one day, palpitations and fatigue the next. A trend chart over the last three months answers "how have you been?" in one glance, and Ebb exports to PDF so you can hand it to your cardiologist or neurologist.
  • Patterns. POTS has triggers — heat, meals, poor sleep, overexertion. Consistent, tiny logs — not perfect ones — are how you spot that flares follow hot days, or that a salt/fluid or medication change actually helped.

The tracker you'll still be using in six months is the one that costs the least energy. Ebb keeps the cost low on purpose:

Common questions

What POTS symptoms can I track?

Anything you name — commonly lightheadedness, palpitations, fatigue, brain fog, nausea, temperature intolerance, and how upright you managed to be that day.

Does Ebb connect to a wearable or read heart rate?

No. Ebb doesn't sync with Apple Health, a heart rate monitor, or any wearable. It's a manual self-rating log: you record what you noticed, 1 to 5. If you want objective heart rate data, keep using your device for that and use Ebb for how you actually felt.

Is Ebb free?

Yes. The free tier lets you track up to 5 symptoms with full daily logging, complete history, 7-day trends, a daily reminder, and CSV export. Premium ($4.99/month or $29.99/year) adds unlimited symptoms, full trend ranges, and doctor-ready PDF reports.

Can I bring this to a cardiologist or autonomic specialist?

Yes. Premium exports a PDF of the period you choose — 30 or 90 days, 6 months, or your full history — with a trend chart per symptom plus your daily ratings and notes (see a real sample). Free exports your full history as CSV.
